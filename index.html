Flapjack Skies Privacy Policy

Developer: Freezing Point
Effective November 6, 2026

This policy covers Flapjack Skies for Android, a one-tap arcade game. It explains what information the game stores, what its optional video ads collect, and the choices you have.

Game progress and settings

Your best score, total points, coins, unlocked and chosen birds, store items, hats and outfits, daily reward streak, theme, sound, music and vibration settings, and your ad privacy choice are stored on your device. There is no game account, sign-in or developer-operated cloud save service, and Freezing Point does not receive your saved progress. Coins are a virtual currency with no cash value, earned only by playing; they can only be spent on cosmetic items in the game's store and cannot be bought with real money, exchanged or withdrawn. The game has no real-money purchases and does not ask for your name, email, date of birth or other contact details.

Optional rewarded ads

After a crash you may choose to watch a video ad to continue your run. Watching an ad is never required to play. Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, Unity's ad mediation service, and the Unity Ads network (Unity Technologies). When the game starts, after the consent check where it is required, the game initializes the advertising service and loads an ad in advance, so advertising data may be processed before you choose to watch an ad.

To deliver and measure ads, the advertising SDK may collect and share with its advertising partners:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available;
  • Device and network information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, carrier and connection type;
  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location (the game does not request GPS location);
  • App and ad interactions, such as ad requests, views, taps and whether an ad was completed;
  • Diagnostics, such as SDK errors and performance information.

This information is used to show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and keep the service secure, and, where you allow it, to show ads that are more relevant to you. On supported Android versions the SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features (Topics and Attribution). Advertising data is transmitted using TLS encryption. The game does not include a separate analytics SDK.

Unity and the advertising partners involved in delivering an ad process this information under their own policies and your choices. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Freezing Point does not sell your saved game progress or support correspondence.

Privacy choices

If you appear to be in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent to personalized ads before any ads are loaded, and passes your answer to Unity LevelPlay. Anyone can review or change this choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized; declining does not necessarily stop all advertising-related processing, because non-personalized ads still require technical data such as the information listed above.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's settings (for example Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Permissions

The game requests internet and network-state access to load ads, the advertising ID permission used by the ad SDK, Android Privacy Sandbox ad permissions where available, and vibration for crash feedback (you can turn vibration off in Settings). Other permissions included by the advertising SDK, such as running scheduled background tasks, support ad delivery and are not used to access your personal files, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Android services

Android backup is enabled. Your device or backup provider may back up or transfer local game data according to your settings. These providers' privacy practices apply to their services.

Retention, deletion and requests

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it, subject to backup and restore settings. To delete local data, open Android Settings > Apps > Flapjack Skies > Storage > Clear data. This resets your progress and preferences. Manage backups separately.

Clearing local data also deletes your coins and store items, because they are saved only on your device. It does not delete information already held by Unity or its advertising partners. They retain advertising data according to their own policies. Use the privacy request options described in Unity's privacy policy for data held by Unity. Freezing Point cannot directly identify or erase advertising records held by these providers.

If you email us, we use your address and the information you send to respond. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and applicable obligations. Contact 4incode@protonmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of information held by Freezing Point, or to ask about privacy rights available in your region. No messages are sent to us automatically by the game.

Younger players

Flapjack Skies is intended for players aged 16 and older and is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can address the request and help direct you to the relevant provider.
